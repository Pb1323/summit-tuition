**Title:** 11+/GCSE online mock-exam platform — 7,400+ questions, 159 full mocks, auto-marking built-in

**Category:** EdTech / SaaS content asset (asset sale, pre-revenue)

**Asking price:** £15,000 (floor £8,000, undisclosed to buyers)

**One-line pitch:** A working Next.js/Postgres platform with 159 hand-authored 11+ and GCSE mock exams (7,426 questions across English, Maths, Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, and GCSE sciences), auto-marking, admin dashboards, and student progress tracking — ready to onboard a tutoring centre's or publisher's own students on day one.

**What's included:**
- Full source code (Next.js 16 / React 19 / TypeScript / Prisma / PostgreSQL), Vercel-deployable
- 159 published mock exams: 48 English, 32 Maths, 28 Verbal Reasoning, 17 Non-Verbal Reasoning, 13 Chemistry, 11 Biology, 10 Physics
- 7,426 individual questions, 46 original comprehension passages, 39 study-notes pages
- Auto-marking engine + admin manual-marking tools for open-response questions
- Student/parent dashboards, progress tracking, mock-unlock/assignment system
- 2-4 weeks of founder handover support (the founder is a GCSE-age student who built and content-authored the entire platform solo)

**What's not included:** the "Summit Tuition" brand/domain is negotiable separately; existing student accounts/data will be excluded from the transfer (buyer onboards their own students — see our GDPR note below, available on request).

**Why now:** the founder's own tutoring roster has wound down to one student, so the content bank and platform are no longer being actively used to capacity — an ideal fit for a tutoring centre, 11+ publisher, or edtech platform wanting to add mock-exam capability without building from scratch.

**Note to buyers:** this is an asset sale, not an ongoing revenue business — priced on replacement cost (an estimated £10k-£20k to rebuild this volume of tested content + platform from scratch), not on a revenue multiple. Full due-diligence pack (code license audit, content-originality statement, GDPR position) available on request once a serious conversation starts.

# Pairing decision: which board gets Maths, which gets English

Task: pick one subject per board for the two sample mocks going to Chris Pearse (Teachitright), based on which board's material for that subject is strongest/most reliable.

## Decision

- **Future Stories / FSCE → Maths sample** (built from Reading School's **Adventure + Beacon** papers' maths content).
- **QUEST → English sample** (built from Part 2's **Creative Comprehension** module, not the adaptive Part 1 English module).

## Reasoning

### Why FSCE for Maths, not QUEST

| | FSCE (Adventure + Beacon) | QUEST (Part 1 Maths) |
|---|---|---|
| Format is fixed/static | Yes — a real paper with a fixed question set | **No — adaptive**; difficulty and even which questions appear change per candidate in real time |
| Topic list found | Specific: number/place value, four operations, factors/primes/squares/cubes, fractions/decimals/percentages, ratio, geometry, statistics, problem-solving | Generic: "arithmetic and number, geometry, measurement, statistics, reasoning" — true but far less specific |
| Timing/structure found | Specific: Adventure 50 Q/50 min (MCQ, incl. maths), Beacon 45 min (written, working shown) | Generic: "20 minutes," no question count, no MCQ/free-response split beyond "a combination" |
| Official primary material | Confirmed to exist (FSCE Familiarisation Guides, hosted directly on multiple school sites) even though this session couldn't fetch their content | None found — QUEST doesn't appear to publish official sample material at all |
| Calculator policy | Not confirmed, but conventionally non-calculator (safe default) | Not confirmed at all |

A static "QUEST Maths sample" would be building against a mechanic (adaptive difficulty) that fundamentally can't be reproduced in a fixed mock — this is a real risk of repeating the exact mistake that got our GL-format content rejected (wrong format for the board), just one layer more subtle. FSCE's Adventure/Beacon papers are conventional fixed-format papers with real topic and timing specifics from multiple independently-sourced, mutually consistent descriptions plus confirmed (if unread) official material — clearly the stronger, safer, more reliable base for a Maths mock.

### Why QUEST for English, not FSCE

This is closer, since FSCE's English material (Adventure's reading/vocab/grammar MCQ, Beacon's missing-letters/short-answer tasks, Discovery's creative writing) is also well documented. But two things tip it to QUEST:

1. **FSCE's strongest, most distinctive English content (Discovery's creative writing) can't be used anyway** — Summit's platform has no free-text/essay auto-marking capability anywhere (see CLAUDE.md Known Limitations), so any FSCE English sample would necessarily be reduced to just the Adventure/Beacon MCQ+short-answer slice, which is comparatively generic (comprehension/vocab/grammar MCQ — similar in kind to what we already produce for GL-style content, the very thing Chris already rejected once).
2. **QUEST's Part 2 Creative Comprehension is both non-adaptive (safe to reproduce statically — see the FSCE-vs-QUEST Maths reasoning above, now working in QUEST's favour) and genuinely distinctive**: a themed set of 6-7 linked sources (map, graph, table, article, image) that the candidate cross-references — a structurally different, more memorable task type than a standard single-passage comprehension test. It's well and consistently documented across Atom Learning's own blog plus multiple independent tutoring guides. As a "sneak peek" meant to re-engage a prospect who's already seen (and rejected) our generic GL-style English content, a structurally distinctive multi-source task is a stronger pitch than another single-passage MCQ paper — and it happens to also be the higher-confidence, higher-reliability material for QUEST specifically, which is what this decision is supposed to be based on.

### Net effect

Each sample mock is built from the higher-confidence, format-safe material available for that board, rather than forcing both boards through the same Maths/English template. The build session should not attempt a QUEST Maths mock (blocked by the adaptive-format problem) or an FSCE Discovery creative-writing mock (blocked by the platform's auto-marking limitation) under this plan.

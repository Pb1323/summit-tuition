Write About a Time...

05

Prompt — choose one

Pick one of these two

1. Write about a time when you had to keep a secret you didn't want to keep.
2. Write about a time when something ordinary suddenly felt dangerous.

Structure

Same structure as before: flashback → present moment → action → dialogue → sensory detail → hook. Then keep going — finish the scene properly, don't stop at the exciting bit.

Checklist — go over it again
  1. ☐ Read it all out loud first.
  2. ☐ Dialogue tags — "asked" / "he" / "she" after speech: lower case, not capital.
  3. ☐ New sentence inside speech marks — starts with a capital. No full stop before "and."
  4. ☐ Was / were — check it matches a singular or plural subject.
  5. ☐ Every sentence has a verb.
  6. ☐ Ending is finished — not notes, not cut off mid-action.

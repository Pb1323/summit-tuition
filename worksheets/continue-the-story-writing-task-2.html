Summit Tuition, Creative Writing Task

Name: Date: Time allowed: 30 minutes

Continue the Story

The scoreboard still showed yesterday's match when Priya let herself into the sports hall before anyone else arrived. She wasn't supposed to have a key, only borrowed one, and she told herself she only wanted five quiet minutes with the court before the tournament crowd filled it. She was halfway through her first serve when she noticed someone already sitting alone in the far corner of the stands.

The extract finishes with Priya noticing she is not alone in the hall. Continue the story, in which you explain who Priya finds in the stands, what happens between them, and how the morning changes Priya's feelings about the tournament ahead.

You might want to include:

  • A description of the other person and why they are there so early
  • Dialogue between Priya and the other person
  • How Priya's feelings about the tournament shift over the scene
  • A clear ending that shows what Priya decides to do next

20 marks for content: imaginative ideas, characterisation, a clear structure with a genuine ending (not a piece that just stops), and how well the tone and style match the opening extract.

5 marks for spelling, punctuation and grammar.

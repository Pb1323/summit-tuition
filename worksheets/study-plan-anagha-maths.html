Week By Week

1

Algebra Foundations + Checking Habit

Week 1
  • Notes: Algebra (/notes/maths/algebra)
  • Covers her exact misses: simplifying expressions (collecting like terms), substitution with negative values, solving inequalities

Short worksheet on these three skills first (Admin → Homework Generator, topic: Algebra). Build in a checking habit from day one: after every answer, she re-substitutes her answer back into the original question to confirm it works, rather than moving straight to the next question. If she's getting them right without hesitating, she does a mixed algebra mock section this week. If she's still pausing or guessing, a second worksheet with fresh questions instead, and the mock waits until week 2.

2

Algebra: Factorising, Rearranging, Forming Equations

Week 2
  • Notes: Algebra (/notes/maths/algebra) — factorising and rearranging formulae subtopics specifically
  • Her other three misses this paper: factorising a common factor, rearranging a formula to make x the subject, forming an equation from a worded problem

Same check-first approach: worksheet, then mock only once she's applying the checking habit from week 1 automatically, not just when reminded. By the end of this week the goal isn't just correct answers — it's consistently checking her own work before calling a question finished.

3

Coordinates & Probability Quick-Hit Review

Week 3
  • Notes: Geometry (/notes/maths/geometry) for coordinate translation; Averages & Statistics (/notes/maths/averages-statistics) for probability of combined events
  • This is a light-touch week, not remedial — only one question was missed in each area

15 minutes on translating a point by a vector (her exact miss), 15 minutes on combined-event probability (e.g. two dice, two independent draws). Goal is making these automatic, not teaching new concepts. Rest of this session: light revision of Decimals and Number Theory (dividing decimals, lowest common multiple) — not weak overall, but both had a single slip worth tidying up.

4

Full Paper

Week 4
  • One full mock across all topics, not just the three weak ones

Arithmetic, Ratio, Geometry, Percentages, Averages and Data Handling are all already 71-100% — this week checks the algebra/checking-habit fixes hold under full-paper conditions, not that anything new needs teaching there. If Algebra is above 75% here, we move on to whatever the paper reveals as the next weak spot. If it's still below that, one more focused week on it before moving forward.

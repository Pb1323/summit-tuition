Creative Writing

Difficult Practice — Free Choice
Candidate name
Date

Choose ONE of the following

1. Write about a time when someone you trusted let you down.

2. Write about a moment when a small decision turned out to matter far more than you expected.

Instructions

  • Plan for 2 minutes before you start writing — decide your ending before you write your opening.
  • Structure: flashback (1 sentence) → present moment → action → dialogue → sensory detail → hook → finish the scene properly.

Techniques required

Analepsis / flashback ×1 sentence
Action sentences ×3
Dialogue exchange (2+ lines) ×1
Sensory detail, 2 senses ×2
Intriguing / withheld detail ×1
Semicolons ×2
Colons ×2
One-sentence paragraph for impact ×1

Your writing

Checklist — go over it again

  1. Read it all out loud first.
  2. Dialogue tags — "asked" / "he" / "she" after speech: lower case, not capital.
  3. New sentence inside speech marks — starts with a capital. No full stop before "and."
  4. Was / were — check it matches a singular or plural subject.
  5. Every sentence has a verb.
  6. No word repeated in the same sentence or the one right after it — swap for a synonym.
  7. At least 3 different sentence openers used — not three sentences in a row starting with "He" / "She" / "The."
  8. Ending is finished — not notes, not cut off mid-action.
